## 🚀 Projects

### [Lumora Retail — Sales & Margin Analysis](https://github.com/JeeraphatSutti/lumora-retail)

Why did gross margin fall, and how do we get it back? An end-to-end analysis of a synthetic 10-year retail business.

- Cleaned and modeled a 7-table retail dataset (266K orders, 10 years), fixing 15 types of data-quality issues across ~74K rows
- Explained why gross margin fell from **27.4% to 25.0%** (2017–2022) and recovered to **27.6%** by 2025, using a margin bridge to split the change into supplier costs, product mix and discounts
- Built a **Streamlit** dashboard and an **Excel** version for business users (SUMIFS dashboard, PivotTable with slicers, Power Query)
- Recommended 4 actions worth an estimated **฿2.9M** in yearly gross profit

**Tech stack:** Python, Pandas, SQL, Streamlit, Excel

### [Retail Customer 360 & Analytics Pipeline](https://github.com/JeeraphatSutti/retail-customer-360)

Who are our best customers, and how do they pay? Customer analytics on the Olist e-commerce dataset.

- Cleaned and consolidated 94,000+ customer and transaction records from 5 sources in **Google BigQuery** (Bronze → Silver → Gold)
- Segmented customers with **RFM analysis** and measured payment preferences to support marketing decisions
- Built an interactive **Looker Studio** dashboard tracking R$ 15.7M in revenue, revenue trends and regional performance
- Orchestrated the pipeline with **Apache Airflow** in Docker

**Tech stack:** SQL, Python, BigQuery, Google Cloud Storage, Looker Studio, Airflow, PySpark, dbt, Docker

### [Movie Box Office ETL Pipeline](https://github.com/JeeraphatSutti/movie-box-office-etl)

End-to-end ETL pipeline that turns ~1M raw movie records into analytics-ready tables.

- Incremental, re-runnable ingestion with deduplication and data-quality checks
- Tracks data lineage (`_ingested_at`, `_source_file`)
- Builds gold-layer aggregates by year, genre and top directors

**Tech stack:** Python, Pandas, Parquet, Airflow, Docker
